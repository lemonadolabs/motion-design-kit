Example · early v2

Ask AI in Creatives, launch clip

25 seconds, 16:9, 4K, no voice-over. It opens by saying this is new, then cuts back and forth between the real product and simple isolated visuals on white (one button, one prompt box, one card). Each use case is the same rhythm: a prompt on white, then the product doing it. Every cut sits on the music's beat.

This is the second storyboard for the example film (Lemonado's "Ask AI in Creatives" launch), kept as a model of the format. Lemonado's product is an ads tool, so the frames show ad cards. For your app, keep the layout and swap the content: in a project tool, frame 6 could be "the task list sorts itself by due date". The final film grew to 42.5s and changed after review (see docs/PLAYBOOK.md §12), so use this page for its layout, not its timings.

What changed from v1

  • New opening that says it's new: "Creatives just got" / "a whole lot simpler." / "You can now [Ask AI]". The last line ends in the real button, which gets pressed, so "you can now ask AI" is both read and seen.
  • Product and simple visuals now alternate: button on white → product opens → prompt on white → product sorts → prompt on white → product flags → cards on white → prompt on white → product builds the board.
  • The big numbered titles are gone. Each use case is introduced by a small label above its prompt box, so the prompt itself is the title.
