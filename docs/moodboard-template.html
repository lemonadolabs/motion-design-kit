3. Use cases

Write at least five candidates, then choose the best three and order them simplest → most valuable. Each one must be shipped today, a job your users recognise, and show a visible change in the product.

Can't fill five? Stop and answer the questions in docs/USE-CASES.md with someone who knows the feature (PM, sales, the person who built it) before going further.

4. Beat by beat: what is on screen

For each chosen use case, decide exactly what the viewer sees. If you can't describe the change in one sentence, it won't read in two seconds.

5. Look

One made-up company or account per film, with data that fits your app: tasks in a project tool, tracks in a music app, invoices in a finance app, messages in a chat app, workouts in a fitness app. Never real customer data.

Use a real brand texture or photo, never a CSS gradient. A soft, light framing with one or two accent colours reads best; a framing that is mostly one strong colour was rejected on the example film.
